Parametric Carbon Optimization

Ribbed concrete shell canopy. Geometry, structural response and embodied carbon in one parametric loop. Research prototype, not for design use.

Solver history

Model and assumptions
  • Section properties are integrated numerically from the shell cross-section, including thickened edge beams.
  • Tapered underside ribs add a smeared T-section to wing stiffness and panel buckling (50% efficiency) and count toward mass.
  • Trees act as point supports of a continuous shell girder (three-moment solution, creep-reduced stiffness).
  • Wing deflection and buckling use shallow-shell surrogates; they rank designs, they do not certify them.
  • Carbon = (shell + tree volume) × density × 0.34 kgCO₂e/kg, a cement-rich HPC/UHPFRC mix, cradle to gate.
  • Optimizer: seeded (1+λ) evolution strategy minimizing carbon with stiffness, utilization and ≤40° slope penalties.
